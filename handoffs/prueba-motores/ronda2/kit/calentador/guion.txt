How does that box on your neighbor's roof give hot showers without a single wire or pump? It runs on one simple rule: hot water rises.
The flat panel is a black metal plate with copper tubes, under a sheet of glass. Sunlight passes through the glass and heats the plate, and the glass traps that heat, like a car parked in the sun.
So the water in the tubes warms up, gets lighter, and rises into the tank sitting above the panel. Colder, heavier water sinks from the bottom of the tank into the panel to take its place.
That loop keeps turning all day with no pump. It's called a thermosiphon.
And because the tank is insulated, the water stays hot into the night.
