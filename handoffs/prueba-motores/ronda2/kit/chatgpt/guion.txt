Why does ChatGPT give a different answer when you ask the exact same question twice? Because it never looks up an answer. It writes one piece at a time.
First, your message is cut into small pieces called tokens. Then the model scores every token it knows, over a hundred thousand of them, and gives each one a probability of coming next.
After "The sky is", blue might get sixty percent, clear twenty, and falling almost zero.
But it doesn't always pick the top one. It rolls weighted dice, so likely words usually win, and sometimes a less likely one sneaks in.
The chosen token is added to the text, and the whole process repeats until the answer is done. So one different roll early on, and the two answers drift apart.
